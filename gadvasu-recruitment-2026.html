Selection procedure

Advt. 04/2026 — Officer, Professor & Scientist

The university prepares a merit score card from qualifications and experience; the top ten eligible candidates per post are called for presentation (Assistant Professor level posts only) and interview, and must score at least 50% marks in the interview. Probation is three years for Sr. No. 3–8 with a service bond, and posts at Sr. No. 5–8 are co-terminus with the plan scheme.

Advt. 03/2026 — Group C (Steno Typist, Clerk, Storekeeper)

  1. Exam-I — Punjabi Language Test (Matric level): 50 marks (40 MCQ + English-to-Punjabi translation), qualifying only, 50% to pass, no negative marking.
  2. Exam-II — Main Examination: 100 marks counting to merit, 40% to qualify, 0.25 negative marking. Storekeeper paper: 30-mark Part-A (general) + 70-mark Part-B (core subject).
  3. Skill test: Clerk — typing at 30 w.p.m. in Punjabi and English with max 8% mistakes (qualifying only); Steno Typist — shorthand dictation at 80 w.p.m. with 15 w.p.m. transcription in both languages (35 marks per language counted to merit).
  4. Merit and document verification: Clerk/Storekeeper merit on Exam-II marks; Steno Typist merit on Exam-II (30%) + shorthand tests (35% + 35%). Ties break on age, then graduation marks, then matric marks.
