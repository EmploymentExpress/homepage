Punjab and Haryana High Court Safai Sewak and Mali Recruitment 2026

The High Court of Punjab and Haryana at Chandigarh published Employment Notice No. 03/SS/ML/HC/2026 for 167 regular support-staff posts: 75 Safai Sewak and 92 Mali.

Application status: The application window stated in the employment notice ran from 9 June 2026 to 30 June 2026 at 11:59 p.m. It is closed. Use the official recruitment page below for any later interview, admit-card, result or corrigendum notice.
