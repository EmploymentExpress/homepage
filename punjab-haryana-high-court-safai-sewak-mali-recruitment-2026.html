Driver eligibility

  • 10+2 pass, with Punjabi or Hindi as a subject in matriculation.
  • Valid Light Motor Vehicle (LMV) driving licence.
  • At least two years of car-driving experience and no conviction for negligent driving.

Driver selection and fee

Shortlisting may be used if required. Provisionally eligible candidates are called for a Practical Test-cum-Interview. Candidates proposed for appointment must complete the High Court’s trial period successfully.

  • General and SC/ST/BC candidates from outside Punjab, Haryana and Chandigarh: ₹1,000
  • SC/ST/BC candidates of Punjab, Haryana and Chandigarh: ₹800
  • Ex-Servicemen: ₹800

High Court Frash Vacancy 2026 — 31 posts

Notice date
28 August 2026
Online application
28 Aug–18 Sep 2026
Age limit
18–35 years
Job location

Frash eligibility

Knowledge of Hindi and the regional language. Age is calculated as on 18 September 2026, subject to the relaxation rules in the detailed notice.

Frash selection and fee

Interview carries 50 marks: 25 marks for suitability to the job and 25 marks for etiquettes and manners. Candidates in merit, up to four times the advertised vacancies category-wise, are called for a qualifying Physical Endurance Test (PET), followed by testimonials checking.

  • PET events: 800-metre race, long jump and high jump.
  • General and SC/ST/BC candidates from outside Punjab, Haryana and Chandigarh: ₹700
  • SC/ST/BC candidates of Punjab, Haryana and Chandigarh: ₹600; Ex-Servicemen: ₹600
