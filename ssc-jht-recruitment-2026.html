How to Check SSC JHT Exam City Slip

  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Candidate Login button.
  3. Enter your Registration Number / User Name and Password / Date of Birth.
  4. Navigate to the Active Admissions / Exam City Status tab for Combined Hindi Translators Examination 2026.
  5. Check your assigned examination city, shift timing, and reporting instructions.
Important: The City Intimation Slip is intended to inform candidates of their exam city in advance to plan travel. The formal Admit Card (Hall Ticket) with exact test centre address will be released 3–4 days prior to the examination.
